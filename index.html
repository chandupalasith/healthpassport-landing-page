FAQ

Questions lab owners ask before switching

How long does it take to get started?

It takes 2 to 3 days. We handle the setup and train your staff online for free.

Will my staff find it easy to use?

Try the demo with your staff first. Once you start, we train them online for free.

Can I use my own report format?

Yes. Reports use your own letterhead, signature and layout.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. Health Passport is web-based, so it runs on a normal computer or even your phone.

Do collecting centres cost extra?

No. Add as many collecting centres as you need at no extra cost.

Can it connect to my analyzers?

Analyzer connection is available. Tell us which machines you use and we'll confirm the details.

Can I add my own reports?

Yes. Every report is fully customizable, and ready-made reports are included to get you started.
