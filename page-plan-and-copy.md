## Deliberately left out

- **Top menu:** it gives visitors ways to leave. The only other link is the existing app login, placed in the footer for current customers.
- **Competitor names:** the price section makes the point, and naming rivals sends readers to compare them.
- **Full feature list:** you have about 45 features in the proposal, and it helps no one here. The demo shows the rest.
- **Analyzer and on-site visit prices:** discussed on the call.
- **"Everything included" and "cheapest":** a lab that needs an analyzer would feel misled, and "cheapest" invites price shopping.
- **Security and backup promises:** on hold until the checks are done.
